# Dr Lesiba J. Rashokeng

Health regulation leader, scholar, author and minister of the Word, based in Pretoria, South Africa.


I work where health regulation, leadership and faith meet: strengthening quality in South Africa's health system, writing on leadership and productivity, and equipping men and boys through ministry.

## What I work on

- **Health regulation and quality** — compliance, enforcement and health system leadership
- **Scholarship** — health leadership and regulatory research
- **Writing** — productivity and Christian books through [Kingdom Scribe Publishing](https://kingdomscribe.co.za)
- **Thought leadership** — the *Pulse on Leadership* newsletter on LinkedIn
- **Ministry** — LJR: Life Worth Living, and the Boys2Men Academy mentorship platform
- **Capacity building** — the Africa Health Leadership Institute
